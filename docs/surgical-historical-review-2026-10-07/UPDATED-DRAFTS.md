## Hemorrhoidectomy

- Resolve preparation scope and add current antithrombotic references without false procedural precision.

### Preparation & antithrombotic coordination

- Do not import the mechanical bowel preparation plus oral-antibiotic recommendation for elective colorectal resection into isolated hemorrhoidectomy. The 2025 Taiwan colorectal-society consensus states that full mechanical preparation is unnecessary for hemorrhoid surgery; a cleansing enema may be considered under the surgical pathway. ([Taiwan Society of Colon and Rectal Surgeons: hemorrhoid consensus (2025)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12572025/); [ASCRS hemorrhoid guideline (2024)](https://www.ascrsu.com/ascrs/view/ASCRS-Evidence-Based-Guidelines-and-Expert-Consensus/3982022/8/Management_of_Hemorrhoids__2024_); [ASRA antithrombotic guideline, fifth edition (2025)](https://rapm.bmj.com/content/early/2025/01/21/rapm-2024-105766))

- Review antiplatelet and anticoagulant indication, exact drug/dose, renal function, procedure extent, hemostasis and any neuraxial/deep-block plan. Agree interruption and resumption with the prescribing/procedural teams rather than applying an automatic stop rule. ([Taiwan Society of Colon and Rectal Surgeons: hemorrhoid consensus (2025)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12572025/); [ASCRS hemorrhoid guideline (2024)](https://www.ascrsu.com/ascrs/view/ASCRS-Evidence-Based-Guidelines-and-Expert-Consensus/3982022/8/Management_of_Hemorrhoids__2024_); [ASRA antithrombotic guideline, fifth edition (2025)](https://rapm.bmj.com/content/early/2025/01/21/rapm-2024-105766))

- The 2024 ASCRS guideline’s detailed antithrombotic discussion concerns rubber-band ligation and acknowledges uncertain hold/restart timing. It must not be repackaged as an excisional-hemorrhoidectomy drug-timing table. ([Taiwan Society of Colon and Rectal Surgeons: hemorrhoid consensus (2025)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12572025/); [ASCRS hemorrhoid guideline (2024)](https://www.ascrsu.com/ascrs/view/ASCRS-Evidence-Based-Guidelines-and-Expert-Consensus/3982022/8/Management_of_Hemorrhoids__2024_); [ASRA antithrombotic guideline, fifth edition (2025)](https://rapm.bmj.com/content/early/2025/01/21/rapm-2024-105766))

- Use current ASRA guidance for neuraxial or deep-block eligibility, separately from the surgeon’s procedural bleeding assessment. The Taiwan consensus also calls for weighing the indication and risk of discontinuation; it does not supply one safe interval for all drugs. ([Taiwan Society of Colon and Rectal Surgeons: hemorrhoid consensus (2025)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12572025/); [ASCRS hemorrhoid guideline (2024)](https://www.ascrsu.com/ascrs/view/ASCRS-Evidence-Based-Guidelines-and-Expert-Consensus/3982022/8/Management_of_Hemorrhoids__2024_); [ASRA antithrombotic guideline, fifth edition (2025)](https://rapm.bmj.com/content/early/2025/01/21/rapm-2024-105766))

## Ostomy Creation / Reversal (Ileostomy / Colostomy)

- Add anastomotic-assessment evidence with explicit ileostomy population and uncertainty.
- Make stoma-viability escalation useful without importing a bedside instrumentation recipe.

### Stoma viability & mechanical complications

- A progressively dusky or black stoma, severe pain, distension or obstructive symptoms needs surgical assessment. Depth and extent of ischemia determine management; do not offer reassurance from a superficial color change without evaluation. ([Stoma Complications: surgical review](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11466528/); [A practical approach to management of high-output stoma: clinical review](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5369744/))

- If the stoma remains, protect it and its appliance from pressure and document output/perfusion concerns in handoff. Stoma type and remaining small-bowel length affect the fluid plan. ([Stoma Complications: surgical review](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11466528/); [A practical approach to management of high-output stoma: clinical review](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5369744/))

- Communicate evolving edema, dusky mucosa or blue/black change promptly and request surgical assessment of depth, not just surface color. The surgical review distinguishes limited superficial ischemia from necrosis extending below the fascia, which warrants operative exploration. ([Stoma Complications: surgical review](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11466528/); [A practical approach to management of high-output stoma: clinical review](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5369744/))

- Endoscopic or other direct assessment of the stoma’s depth is a surgical-team procedure. Do not use this brief anesthesia reference as instructions for blind instrumentation, and do not delay escalation because some superficial changes can be observed. ([Stoma Complications: surgical review](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11466528/); [A practical approach to management of high-output stoma: clinical review](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5369744/))

### After reversal: bowel function & anastomosis

- Expect bowel function to change after restoration of continuity. Distinguish expected recovery from persistent vomiting/distension, worsening pain or systemic deterioration that may indicate obstruction, ileus, leak or infection. ([Stoma Complications: clinical review (2024 issue)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11466528/); [ASCRS/SAGES: enhanced recovery after colon and rectal surgery (2023)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9839829/); [Anastomotic evaluation before ileostomy closure: 2025 systematic review](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12159718/))

- Use the relevant colorectal recovery pathway when clinically appropriate, but do not force oral intake or discharge in a patient whose perfusion, bowel function or laboratory abnormalities remain concerning. ([Stoma Complications: clinical review (2024 issue)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11466528/); [ASCRS/SAGES: enhanced recovery after colon and rectal surgery (2023)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9839829/); [Anastomotic evaluation before ileostomy closure: 2025 systematic review](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12159718/))

- Before reversal of a defunctioning ileostomy, confirm that the surgical team has assessed the downstream anastomosis and addressed any prior leak, sinus or stricture. The operation name or passage of time alone does not establish integrity. ([Stoma Complications: clinical review (2024 issue)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11466528/); [ASCRS/SAGES: enhanced recovery after colon and rectal surgery (2023)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9839829/); [Anastomotic evaluation before ileostomy closure: 2025 systematic review](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12159718/))

- A 2025 systematic review supports considering endoscopy and digital rectal examination, with water-soluble contrast studies useful when a leak is suspected or in selected higher-risk contexts. Evidence was very low certainty and no randomized trials were identified; no single mandatory test or “negative test guarantees healing” rule is justified. ([Stoma Complications: clinical review (2024 issue)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11466528/); [ASCRS/SAGES: enhanced recovery after colon and rectal surgery (2023)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9839829/); [Anastomotic evaluation before ileostomy closure: 2025 systematic review](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12159718/))

- That evidence concerns adults with distal colonic/pelvic anastomoses and defunctioning ileostomies. Do not apply its test-performance estimates to every colostomy reversal or other reconstruction. ([Stoma Complications: clinical review (2024 issue)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11466528/); [ASCRS/SAGES: enhanced recovery after colon and rectal surgery (2023)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9839829/); [Anastomotic evaluation before ileostomy closure: 2025 systematic review](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12159718/))

## Sleeve Gastrectomy

- Restore the explicit, conditional TOF recovery detail identified in the historical audit.
- Supplement historical PONV gap with scoped bariatric guidance, not a fixed regimen.

### Extubation, CPAP/BiPAP & postoperative monitoring

- Plan extubation as deliberately as induction. Use head-up positioning, adequate preoxygenation, awake airway control and objectively confirmed neuromuscular recovery; have equipment and assistance ready if re-intubation is necessary. ([SOBA 2025: airway management in patients living with obesity](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12351209/); [BJA Education 2022: anaesthesia for bariatric surgery](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC9125419/); [ERAS Society: bariatric perioperative care, 2021 update](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00268-021-06394-9); [APSF summary of ASA neuromuscular blockade guidelines (2023)](https://www.apsf.org/article/new-practice-guidelines-for-neuromuscular-blockade/))

- Continue prescribed home CPAP/BiPAP in the postoperative period when indicated. For increased desaturation risk, consider extubation directly to CPAP or HFNO; OHS or persistent hypoxemia may warrant bilevel/noninvasive ventilatory support and a higher-acuity setting. ([SOBA 2025: airway management in patients living with obesity](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12351209/); [BJA Education 2022: anaesthesia for bariatric surgery](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC9125419/); [ERAS Society: bariatric perioperative care, 2021 update](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00268-021-06394-9); [APSF summary of ASA neuromuscular blockade guidelines (2023)](https://www.apsf.org/article/new-practice-guidelines-for-neuromuscular-blockade/))

- CPAP is not automatically prohibited after bariatric surgery and is not mandatory for every patient. Match support to OSA/OHS, oxygenation, ventilation and surgical/clinical circumstances; persistent deterioration requires escalation, not repeated mask adjustments alone. ([SOBA 2025: airway management in patients living with obesity](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12351209/); [BJA Education 2022: anaesthesia for bariatric surgery](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC9125419/); [ERAS Society: bariatric perioperative care, 2021 update](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00268-021-06394-9); [APSF summary of ASA neuromuscular blockade guidelines (2023)](https://www.apsf.org/article/new-practice-guidelines-for-neuromuscular-blockade/))

- Monitor for obstruction, hypoventilation, residual block and opioid-related respiratory depression. Supplemental oxygen can conceal impaired ventilation; a reassuring oxygen saturation alone does not exclude hypercapnia or ventilatory failure. ([SOBA 2025: airway management in patients living with obesity](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12351209/); [BJA Education 2022: anaesthesia for bariatric surgery](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC9125419/); [ERAS Society: bariatric perioperative care, 2021 update](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00268-021-06394-9); [APSF summary of ASA neuromuscular blockade guidelines (2023)](https://www.apsf.org/article/new-practice-guidelines-for-neuromuscular-blockade/))

- Choose PACU, monitored ward or higher-acuity care based on respiratory events, OSA/OHS, comorbidities, opioid needs and recovery, rather than BMI alone. Continue head-up recovery, mobilization and coordinated VTE prevention. ([SOBA 2025: airway management in patients living with obesity](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12351209/); [BJA Education 2022: anaesthesia for bariatric surgery](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC9125419/); [ERAS Society: bariatric perioperative care, 2021 update](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00268-021-06394-9); [APSF summary of ASA neuromuscular blockade guidelines (2023)](https://www.apsf.org/article/new-practice-guidelines-for-neuromuscular-blockade/))

- When a neuromuscular blocker has been given, confirm quantitative recovery at the adductor pollicis with a train-of-four ratio of at least 0.9 before extubation. This does not mandate blockade in every case, and clinical strength or elapsed time alone does not prove recovery. ([SOBA 2025: airway management in patients living with obesity](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12351209/); [BJA Education 2022: anaesthesia for bariatric surgery](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC9125419/); [ERAS Society: bariatric perioperative care, 2021 update](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00268-021-06394-9); [APSF summary of ASA neuromuscular blockade guidelines (2023)](https://www.apsf.org/article/new-practice-guidelines-for-neuromuscular-blockade/))

### PONV: prevention, rescue & surgical reassessment

- Bariatric ERAS supports multimodal PONV prevention, reduction of perioperative opioid exposure and consideration of propofol-based TIVA when appropriate. This is bariatric guidance, not proof of one uniquely effective sleeve- or bypass-specific drug combination. ([ERAS bariatric recommendations: 2021 update](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00268-021-06394-9); [BJA Education 2022: anaesthesia for bariatric surgery](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC9125419/); [ASMBS: bariatric procedure anatomy and differences](https://asmbs.org/patients/bariatric-surgery-procedures/); [StatPearls: anesthetic considerations in bariatric surgery](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK603748/))

- The guideline describes combining agents from three different antiemetic classes. Select the actual agents and timing for contraindications, interactions and patient risk; avoid duplicate dosing and account for sedation, QT risk and glycemic effects where relevant. ([ERAS bariatric recommendations: 2021 update](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00268-021-06394-9); [BJA Education 2022: anaesthesia for bariatric surgery](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC9125419/); [ASMBS: bariatric procedure anatomy and differences](https://asmbs.org/patients/bariatric-surgery-procedures/); [StatPearls: anesthetic considerations in bariatric surgery](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK603748/))

- Document a rescue plan and reassess persistent vomiting, poor intake or vomiting accompanied by pain, tachycardia or systemic deterioration. Do not repeatedly treat nausea without considering a surgical complication. ([ERAS bariatric recommendations: 2021 update](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00268-021-06394-9); [BJA Education 2022: anaesthesia for bariatric surgery](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC9125419/); [ASMBS: bariatric procedure anatomy and differences](https://asmbs.org/patients/bariatric-surgery-procedures/); [StatPearls: anesthetic considerations in bariatric surgery](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK603748/))

## Roux-en-Y Gastric Bypass (RYGB)

- Restore the explicit, conditional TOF recovery detail identified in the historical audit.
- Supplement historical PONV gap with scoped bariatric guidance, not a fixed regimen.
- Reconcile the historical RYGB NSAID question with ASMBS risk and prophylaxis guidance.

### Analgesia, nausea prevention & recovery

- Favor multimodal, opioid-sparing analgesia. Local infiltration or regional techniques can reduce opioid needs; consider organ function, maximum doses and the procedure-specific surgical plan. ([ERAS Society: bariatric perioperative care, 2021 update](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00268-021-06394-9); [BJA Education 2022: anaesthesia for bariatric surgery](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC9125419/); [StatPearls: anesthetic considerations in bariatric surgery](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK603748/); [ASMBS: bariatric procedure anatomy and differences](https://asmbs.org/patients/bariatric-surgery-procedures/); [ASMBS marginal-ulcer risk and prophylaxis review (2024)](https://asmbs.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/American-Society-for-Metabolic-and-Bariatric-Surgery-literature-review-on-risk-factors-screening-recommendations-and-prophylaxis-for-marginal-ulcers-after-metabolic-and-bariatric-surgery.pdf))

- Use risk-based multimodal PONV prophylaxis and a rescue plan. Vomiting is particularly undesirable after upper-GI surgery and should not be dismissed when accompanied by pain, tachycardia or inability to tolerate intake. ([ERAS Society: bariatric perioperative care, 2021 update](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00268-021-06394-9); [BJA Education 2022: anaesthesia for bariatric surgery](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC9125419/); [StatPearls: anesthetic considerations in bariatric surgery](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK603748/); [ASMBS: bariatric procedure anatomy and differences](https://asmbs.org/patients/bariatric-surgery-procedures/); [ASMBS marginal-ulcer risk and prophylaxis review (2024)](https://asmbs.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/American-Society-for-Metabolic-and-Bariatric-Surgery-literature-review-on-risk-factors-screening-recommendations-and-prophylaxis-for-marginal-ulcers-after-metabolic-and-bariatric-surgery.pdf))

- NSAID suitability differs by procedure and patient. In Roux-en-Y patients, marginal-ulcer risk is a specific concern; do not automatically transfer a generic NSAID-based pathway to a bypass patient. ([ERAS Society: bariatric perioperative care, 2021 update](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00268-021-06394-9); [BJA Education 2022: anaesthesia for bariatric surgery](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC9125419/); [StatPearls: anesthetic considerations in bariatric surgery](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK603748/); [ASMBS: bariatric procedure anatomy and differences](https://asmbs.org/patients/bariatric-surgery-procedures/); [ASMBS marginal-ulcer risk and prophylaxis review (2024)](https://asmbs.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/American-Society-for-Metabolic-and-Bariatric-Surgery-literature-review-on-risk-factors-screening-recommendations-and-prophylaxis-for-marginal-ulcers-after-metabolic-and-bariatric-surgery.pdf))

- Resume oral intake and mobilization according to the bariatric pathway. Persistent intolerance, dehydration or nutritional problems need follow-up rather than repeated symptomatic treatment alone. ([ERAS Society: bariatric perioperative care, 2021 update](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00268-021-06394-9); [BJA Education 2022: anaesthesia for bariatric surgery](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC9125419/); [StatPearls: anesthetic considerations in bariatric surgery](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK603748/); [ASMBS: bariatric procedure anatomy and differences](https://asmbs.org/patients/bariatric-surgery-procedures/); [ASMBS marginal-ulcer risk and prophylaxis review (2024)](https://asmbs.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/American-Society-for-Metabolic-and-Bariatric-Surgery-literature-review-on-risk-factors-screening-recommendations-and-prophylaxis-for-marginal-ulcers-after-metabolic-and-bariatric-surgery.pdf))

- The ASMBS review identifies NSAID exposure as a marginal-ulcer risk factor after RYGB. A generic opioid-sparing NSAID pathway should therefore not be imported without an explicit bariatric-team assessment of alternatives and gastrointestinal, renal and bleeding risks. ([ERAS Society: bariatric perioperative care, 2021 update](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00268-021-06394-9); [BJA Education 2022: anaesthesia for bariatric surgery](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC9125419/); [StatPearls: anesthetic considerations in bariatric surgery](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK603748/); [ASMBS: bariatric procedure anatomy and differences](https://asmbs.org/patients/bariatric-surgery-procedures/); [ASMBS marginal-ulcer risk and prophylaxis review (2024)](https://asmbs.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/American-Society-for-Metabolic-and-Bariatric-Surgery-literature-review-on-risk-factors-screening-recommendations-and-prophylaxis-for-marginal-ulcers-after-metabolic-and-bariatric-surgery.pdf))

- ASMBS recommends postoperative PPI prophylaxis for at least three months after RYGB, with possible longer treatment for identified risks. Confirm the patient’s actual prescription and adherence; the review does not establish a universally safe NSAID dose or duration, and PPI therapy is not proof that NSAID exposure is risk-free. ([ERAS Society: bariatric perioperative care, 2021 update](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00268-021-06394-9); [BJA Education 2022: anaesthesia for bariatric surgery](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC9125419/); [StatPearls: anesthetic considerations in bariatric surgery](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK603748/); [ASMBS: bariatric procedure anatomy and differences](https://asmbs.org/patients/bariatric-surgery-procedures/); [ASMBS marginal-ulcer risk and prophylaxis review (2024)](https://asmbs.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/American-Society-for-Metabolic-and-Bariatric-Surgery-literature-review-on-risk-factors-screening-recommendations-and-prophylaxis-for-marginal-ulcers-after-metabolic-and-bariatric-surgery.pdf))

## Burn Wound Debridement / Excision

- Supply current adult burn transfusion guidance with hemorrhage, brain-injury and ACS exceptions.
- Resolve the existence-of-evidence gap while retaining TXA and cell-salvage uncertainty.
- Update timing evidence without inventing adult-only certainty or an obligatory window.
- Update evidence scope after supplementation.

### Adult major burns: transfusion evidence & exceptions

- The 2025 ABA blood-product guideline addresses hospitalized adults with acute burns; its restrictive RBC recommendation specifically concerns burns of at least 20% TBSA. The studied restrictive approach gives RBCs for a pretransfusion hemoglobin below 7 g/dL to maintain hemoglobin at least 7 g/dL, rather than maintaining at least 10 g/dL. ([ABA blood-product transfusion guideline (2025 full text)](https://doi.org/10.1093/jbcr/iraf021))

- These thresholds do not apply to significant bleeding or hemodynamic instability in or outside the operating room. Active hemorrhage requires clinical resuscitation; do not wait for a laboratory threshold or delay source control. ([ABA blood-product transfusion guideline (2025 full text)](https://doi.org/10.1093/jbcr/iraf021))

- The guideline excludes coexisting acute brain injury from that restrictive recommendation and notes that a less restrictive approach may be appropriate in acute coronary syndrome. Assess the actual physiology and comorbidity rather than using burn size alone. ([ABA blood-product transfusion guideline (2025 full text)](https://doi.org/10.1093/jbcr/iraf021))

- ABA recommends TEG or ROTEM to guide perioperative blood-product therapy during major burn debridement. Follow the local assay and hemorrhage pathway; no universal numerical viscoelastic trigger or fixed plasma/platelet recipe is supplied here. ([ABA blood-product transfusion guideline (2025 full text)](https://doi.org/10.1093/jbcr/iraf021))

- ABA could not recommend for or against a fixed 1:1:1 RBC:plasma:platelet strategy for the studied burn-excision outcomes. This uncertainty is not an instruction to withhold the institution’s massive-hemorrhage response during uncontrolled bleeding. ([ABA blood-product transfusion guideline (2025 full text)](https://doi.org/10.1093/jbcr/iraf021))

### TXA, cell salvage & limits of generalization

- ABA gives a weak conditional recommendation to consider perioperative IV TXA for burn wound debridement, ideally informed by viscoelastic testing and the lysis phenotype. It is not a mandate for every graft or a universal dose instruction; thrombosis-related safety and applicability to early excision remain uncertain. ([ABA blood-product transfusion guideline (2025 full text)](https://doi.org/10.1093/jbcr/iraf021); [Tranburn randomized trial (2025)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12755343/); [Cell salvage in contaminated fields: scoping review (2026)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12768276/))

- The separate Tranburn randomized trial found no additional blood-loss or transfusion benefit from TXA alongside topical epinephrine in its limited burn-graft surgery population. It did not exclude a possible effect in more extensive excisions; the result does not prove that TXA always works or never works in burn surgery. ([ABA blood-product transfusion guideline (2025 full text)](https://doi.org/10.1093/jbcr/iraf021); [Tranburn randomized trial (2025)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12755343/); [Cell salvage in contaminated fields: scoping review (2026)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12768276/))

- Routine cell salvage is not established by these sources. A 2026 scoping review found bacterial contamination in all samples in two small burn-excision series, both rated poor quality; it could not establish burn-specific reinfusion safety. Any use requires a case-specific transfusion/surgical plan, not an assumption that washing or filtration guarantees a safe product. ([ABA blood-product transfusion guideline (2025 full text)](https://doi.org/10.1093/jbcr/iraf021); [Tranburn randomized trial (2025)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12755343/); [Cell salvage in contaminated fields: scoping review (2026)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12768276/))

### Excision timing: physiology before a clock rule

- Coordinate excision timing with resuscitation, hemodynamic stability, depth/extent, donor sites, infection concerns and available blood, anesthesia and warming support. Do not state that excision within a fixed 72-hour window guarantees lower mortality. ([Burn excision timing: systematic review (2026; not adult-only)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13309668/))

- A 2026 systematic review associated early excision with shorter hospitalization but did not demonstrate a consistent mortality benefit; timing definitions and studies were heterogeneous, and its population was not explicitly adult-only. This is not proof of equivalence or a replacement for adult burn-service judgment. ([Burn excision timing: systematic review (2026; not adult-only)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13309668/))

### Adult major burns: transfusion evidence & exceptions

- The 2025 ABA blood-product guideline addresses hospitalized adults with acute burns; its restrictive RBC recommendation specifically concerns burns of at least 20% TBSA. The studied restrictive approach gives RBCs for a pretransfusion hemoglobin below 7 g/dL to maintain hemoglobin at least 7 g/dL, rather than maintaining at least 10 g/dL. ([ABA blood-product transfusion guideline (2025 full text)](https://doi.org/10.1093/jbcr/iraf021))

- These thresholds do not apply to significant bleeding or hemodynamic instability in or outside the operating room. Active hemorrhage requires clinical resuscitation; do not wait for a laboratory threshold or delay source control. ([ABA blood-product transfusion guideline (2025 full text)](https://doi.org/10.1093/jbcr/iraf021))

- The guideline excludes coexisting acute brain injury from that restrictive recommendation and notes that a less restrictive approach may be appropriate in acute coronary syndrome. Assess the actual physiology and comorbidity rather than using burn size alone. ([ABA blood-product transfusion guideline (2025 full text)](https://doi.org/10.1093/jbcr/iraf021))

- ABA recommends TEG or ROTEM to guide perioperative blood-product therapy during major burn debridement. Follow the local assay and hemorrhage pathway; no universal numerical viscoelastic trigger or fixed plasma/platelet recipe is supplied here. ([ABA blood-product transfusion guideline (2025 full text)](https://doi.org/10.1093/jbcr/iraf021))

- ABA could not recommend for or against a fixed 1:1:1 RBC:plasma:platelet strategy for the studied burn-excision outcomes. This uncertainty is not an instruction to withhold the institution’s massive-hemorrhage response during uncontrolled bleeding. ([ABA blood-product transfusion guideline (2025 full text)](https://doi.org/10.1093/jbcr/iraf021))

### TXA, cell salvage & limits of generalization

- ABA gives a weak conditional recommendation to consider perioperative IV TXA for burn wound debridement, ideally informed by viscoelastic testing and the lysis phenotype. It is not a mandate for every graft or a universal dose instruction; thrombosis-related safety and applicability to early excision remain uncertain. ([ABA blood-product transfusion guideline (2025 full text)](https://doi.org/10.1093/jbcr/iraf021); [Tranburn randomized trial (2025)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12755343/); [Cell salvage in contaminated fields: scoping review (2026)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12768276/))

- The separate Tranburn randomized trial found no additional blood-loss or transfusion benefit from TXA alongside topical epinephrine in its limited burn-graft surgery population. It did not exclude a possible effect in more extensive excisions; the result does not prove that TXA always works or never works in burn surgery. ([ABA blood-product transfusion guideline (2025 full text)](https://doi.org/10.1093/jbcr/iraf021); [Tranburn randomized trial (2025)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12755343/); [Cell salvage in contaminated fields: scoping review (2026)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12768276/))

- Routine cell salvage is not established by these sources. A 2026 scoping review found bacterial contamination in all samples in two small burn-excision series, both rated poor quality; it could not establish burn-specific reinfusion safety. Any use requires a case-specific transfusion/surgical plan, not an assumption that washing or filtration guarantees a safe product. ([ABA blood-product transfusion guideline (2025 full text)](https://doi.org/10.1093/jbcr/iraf021); [Tranburn randomized trial (2025)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12755343/); [Cell salvage in contaminated fields: scoping review (2026)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12768276/))

### Excision timing: physiology before a clock rule

- Coordinate excision timing with resuscitation, hemodynamic stability, depth/extent, donor sites, infection concerns and available blood, anesthesia and warming support. Do not state that excision within a fixed 72-hour window guarantees lower mortality. ([Burn excision timing: systematic review (2026; not adult-only)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13309668/))

- A 2026 systematic review associated early excision with shorter hospitalization but did not demonstrate a consistent mortality benefit; timing definitions and studies were heterogeneous, and its population was not explicitly adult-only. This is not proof of equivalence or a replacement for adult burn-service judgment. ([Burn excision timing: systematic review (2026; not adult-only)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13309668/))

## Escharotomy

- Add local-analgesia and postrelease reassessment details to the split escharotomy route.

### Procedure-specific anesthesia priorities

- Escharotomy releases constricting skin without intentionally opening the deep fascial compartments. Indications arise from evolving circulatory or respiratory compromise, not merely the visual presence of a burn. ([StatPearls: Escharotomy](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/sites/books/NBK482120/); [Anesthesiology: acute and perioperative care of burn injury](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4844008/))

- Distal pulse loss is a late finding; surgical assessment integrates perfusion, Doppler and the clinical course. Ventilatory restriction may occur with significant chest or abdominal wall eschar. ([StatPearls: Escharotomy](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/sites/books/NBK482120/); [Anesthesiology: acute and perioperative care of burn injury](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4844008/))

- Choose anesthesia and location to match urgency and physiologic stability, with airway/monitoring support immediately available. Reassess the result and arrange ongoing limb or respiratory surveillance. ([StatPearls: Escharotomy](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/sites/books/NBK482120/); [Anesthesiology: acute and perioperative care of burn injury](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4844008/))

- Local anesthetic is needed for unburnt skin into which the release extends. Sedation may be used, and GA is not invariably required, but pain, associated injury, respiratory compromise and the ability to rescue the airway determine the actual plan; do not equate eschar with an entirely painless procedure. ([StatPearls: Escharotomy](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/sites/books/NBK482120/); [Anesthesiology: acute and perioperative care of burn injury](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4844008/))

- Reassess distal perfusion or respiratory mechanics during and after release. Persistent compromise needs urgent surgical reassessment for incomplete release, a deep-compartment problem or another cause; neither a promised airway-pressure drop nor absent pulse is an adequate single decision rule. ([StatPearls: Escharotomy](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/sites/books/NBK482120/); [Anesthesiology: acute and perioperative care of burn injury](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4844008/))
